About the Author

Grace can rewrite a story.

Joshua White is a Christian author whose writing is rooted in the conviction that our past does not have to define our future. After seasons of failure, wandering, repentance, and spiritual renewal, Joshua returned to his faith with a deeper understanding of grace, responsibility, and the transforming power of Jesus Christ.

His work speaks especially to people who want to date, love, lead, heal, and prepare for marriage in a way that honors God. Rather than presenting perfection, Joshua writes from lived experience—pointing readers toward biblical truth, honest self-examination, and a life surrendered to Christ.
